# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Map zoom: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use + and − (and the arrow keys, + , − and 0 on the focused map). At 2× towns appear and small clusters fan out; at 4× dungeons and every character's name show, the zone in the centre is named in the top bar and the others dim. The map opens with the land centred, Azeroth / Kalimdor / Eastern Kingdoms fly there, and the switch follows when you pan to the other continent. Dragged past the edge it gives way only partly and springs back. A link to a character flies to it; "See them drop in on the map" in a session report drops that upload's new sightings in with a ripple.
- Sign-in gets its look: the community's name and "Where is everyone in Azeroth tonight?" over the drifting map, the Battle.net button and "We only read your BattleTag, nothing else". After Battle.net a short "Checking the guest list… Back from Battle.net as …" and then the map (or the page you opened). Someone not on the list sees "The gate stays closed" with their BattleTag, who to ask, and buttons to use another Battle.net account or sign out; when Battle.net doesn't answer, "Couldn't reach Battle.net" with "Try again".
- The footer credit is gone from every page; the credits come back in another place later.
- The character page: everything about one character. Name, level, race, class, faction and ruleset, who plays it, when it was last played and how fresh the data is; levels to 60 and the dungeon that fits the group; item level, role and main tree; talent bars; professions with a link to compare them with the group; the quest log; a small map of where it was last seen, with who else is there and a link to the map; and the player's other characters. Opens from the roster and the professions page.
- The world map on the home page: every friend's character at the zone where the addon last saw them, styled by how fresh the sighting is (glowing and pulsing when new, a ring after 3 days, dashed after a week), zones tinted gold by their latest sighting. Several characters in a zone form a cluster that fans out (or lists them, from six), with a badge when someone is in a dungeon. Point at or focus a character for a card with level, race, class, spec, zone, when seen, player and item level; click to select it (the link keeps it). Switch between Azeroth, Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms in the top bar.
- Professions: who can craft what across the group. A summary of the professions someone has at 225 or higher (covered), at 175–224 (thin) and below (gaps); a card per profession with everyone's skill bar, the room left until the current cap and the best starred; and a table of every character's professions. A card's title opens the roster filtered on that profession, a name opens the character. Recipe search follows once the addon shares known recipes.
- The roster as a table: every character on one line (player, level, race and class, role, talents, item level, professions, last played, last seen), sorted by clicking a column header (again to reverse), optionally grouped by friend, with the same search and filters as the cards; switching between cards and table keeps them. Your own characters are highlighted. On a phone the table shows as the card list, with filters and sort in a sheet.
- Session report: after an upload, and from every line of the upload history, a report of what changed: levels gained, skill gains (with "CAP REACHED"), new characters, new sightings, item level gains, and who sent data and how fresh it was. It rises in with the bars growing and the level ups shining (still without motion; "Replay" plays it again). Everyone signed in can open a report; a rejected upload, or one that changed nothing, says why.
- The roster: every friend with their characters as cards (class, level, race, talents, role, faction, professions, when last played, where last seen, item level), how fresh each friend's data is, who is around which level with the typical dungeon ranges, and quick answers (healers, tanks, level 30–40, level 60). Search, filter by class, role, level and profession, and sort; the choice is in the address, so a link shares it and Back undoes it.
- Upload page: drop `CommunitiesForever.lua` anywhere on the page or choose it (phones too). While it is read the page counts the characters and friends in it, then opens what the upload changed. A wrong file (another addon's), a damaged file, a file from a newer addon, the same data as before, someone else's file, a file of another community and a file over 25 MB each get a plain explanation and nothing changes. The history lists everyone's uploads with who sent them, what changed ("5 levels gained · 4 skill gains · 1 new character") or why it was rejected; times are shown in your own time zone.
- Atmosphere: drifting fog and light shafts on the map page, the drifting map with fog and embers behind sign-in, a quiet glow on the other pages. A "Motion" switch in the account menu turns all motion off (or on); it follows your system setting until you flip it and is remembered on this device.
- The account menu and the centre card are blurred over what's behind them again (the build had dropped the blur for Chrome), and screen readers hear the account button as "Account" plus the BattleTag shown.
- The map page sits on the darker map background.
- The site runs as a Docker image on TrueNAS behind nginx at https://wow.vestras.nl; `/api/health` tells whether it is up and which version runs.
- Sign in with Battle.net. Only the admin, members of the community (everyone named in an accepted upload) and guests on the admin's guest list get in; everyone else sees "You're not on the list". A blocked BattleTag is out at once.
- Uploads are accepted only for your own file of the same community: someone else's file is rejected as "not your file", one of another community as "other community".
- The site gets its look: the "Night over Azeroth" colours and type, a top bar with the community's name, Map / Roster / Professions, how old the data is, Upload and the account menu with sign out, a tab bar on phones, the footer credit, and a banner when the last upload is a week old or more.
- Map data: Thousand Needles is dry canyon again instead of the Cataclysm lake; Mount Hyjal, The Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Zephras Isle are zones on the map, so characters there (and in Krol'dok Stronghold, The Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit) are placed on it instead of "elsewhere".
