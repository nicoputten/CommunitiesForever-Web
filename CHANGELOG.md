# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Roster: a "Next run" card above the level spread; the dungeon bands sit in at most four rows (most fits first, "Show all" for the rest), say how many fit and how many runs are planned, and open a popover with who's missing, the planned runs and Plan a run (a sheet on a phone). The dot tooltip no longer gets cut off at the top and keeps each character on one line.
- Asks ("who's up right now") are kept on the site: from the game, also when they aren't posted on Discord, and from Discord's `/ask` once the bot has it. Friends can answer them in Discord for 10 minutes, and open asks go to the game through the uploader.
- Runs v2, part 1: answer a run with any of your characters and pick the role apart from it (roles your class can't play are off, a full role makes you reserve), in a panel that opens from Answer (a sheet on a phone). The run page shows the time large with a countdown, each slot with the character's fit, and Could fill, Maybe and Reserve. The organiser (and the admin) move people from a slot's ⋯ menu, also swapping two people; organisers can bench themselves or leave the group and stay organiser. Plan a run picks Lead as the same way, with the role.
- Every dropdown on the site is now the site's own (Plan a run, your answer's character, the Discord page): a search from 8 options, groups, and on a phone a sheet from the bottom. The date and time fields are the site's own too, without the browser's icons, and pages no longer jump sideways when the scrollbar appears.
- Runs from the game: runs planned and answers given in game (with the coming addon version) reach the site with your next upload, "Ask now" with "Also post on Discord" and Discord pings from the game reach the bot, and past runs say "Ran it · 3 of 5 signed-up came" when an upload's run log shows it, or "Didn't happen". The session report and the upload history say "N run answers". The site reads the coming addon's new file format.
- Card images for the Discord bot: the weekly recap's header (the award winner or the busiest zone on the map), the Zone of the week map, milestone cards (level 60, a new character at 60, a profession at 300) and where a character was last seen, in the site's own style.
- The weekly recap: every week at the recap time (the Discord page, Sunday 13:00 by default) the site puts together the week of the community (a headline, the numbers, "<community> of the week", four cards that change from week to week and a challenge) and the bot posts it. The admin sees the coming one under Discord › Preview next recap.
- Runs: one rule for how a character's level fits a run ("fits", "+3 over", "carry · +12", "4 below"); up to 5 levels below may still join, further below only Maybe. Each sign-up has its own role, one the class can play. An I'm in for a full role becomes reserve and moves up first when a slot frees. The organiser (or the admin) can move a sign-up to another role, to reserve, or off the run; the friend gets a quiet Discord notice.
- The uploader app can fetch the upcoming runs (the next 14 days) to show them in the game, once the app and the addon support it.

## v0.3.0 (2026-10-27)
- Runs (top bar › Runs, a fourth tab on a phone): plan a dungeon run for a day and time, leading as one of your characters; friends answer I'm in, Maybe or Can't here or in Discord. Five slots (tank, healer, three damage) fill from each character's role; when a role is full, I'm in waits as Maybe, first in line, and moves up when someone drops out. Times show in your own time zone ("19:30 your time · 20:30 for Nico"). The list shows this week, later and past runs, filtered on the runs you can join; the count next to Runs is how many you can join and haven't answered. The organiser edits day, time and note or deletes the run; the admin can delete any. Scarlet Monastery's wings are runs of their own. On a phone the answer buttons stay at the bottom, and an answer that didn't go through is kept and sent again.
- Uploads larger than 10 MB arrive whole again: from the upload page, from the uploader app and the uploader's own releases (they were cut at 10 MB and refused).
- Discord page: the channel and mention dropdowns list the Discord server's channels and roles once the bot has reported them. A picked channel or role that was deleted on Discord shows as removed until you pick another.

## v0.2.0 (2026-10-27)
- The site has its own icon: the shield in the browser tab, on a phone's home screen ("Add to home screen" opens it like an app, named after the community) and on iOS.
- The Discord page (top bar and account menu › Discord): link your Discord account so the bot knows which characters are yours (the site only asks Discord for your username), or unlink it, and set your time zone, which the browser fills in after you sign in. The admin also sets what the bot posts (milestones, runs, weekly recap), the recap time, the runs options and reminder time, and sees who linked. The admin makes and revokes the bot's token there, sees whether the bot is online and sends a test post. Picking channels and roles follows with the bot.
- The site can feed the Discord bot: milestones from every upload (every 10 levels, level 60, a profession at its cap, a new character), handed to the bot through its own API with its own token. The bot follows in its own repo.
- Roster, "Who is around which level": the track shows only dots; hover, focus or tap a dot for the name, level and player. Characters within two levels of each other share one dot with a number, and your own characters have a gold ring. Dungeon bands only say how many fit ("SM Armory 32–42 · 4 fit"), no names. Scarlet Monastery's Graveyard, Library, Armory and Cathedral are separate dungeons with their own level ranges, on the roster and in the character page's "Fits …".
- "Download for Windows" on the uploader page works once the uploader app is released: it shows the version and size and downloads the installer. Installed apps update themselves from the site.
- The uploader page (top bar › Uploader): what the Windows uploader does and how to set it up in three steps, the connection request when the app asks to connect (check the code, then Allow or Deny), your uploaders with when they connected and last uploaded and a Revoke button, and a token you can create by hand (shown once, with Copy). The manual upload stays one click away under "Upload manually". Uploads the app sends say "via" and the device's name in the upload history. The download follows once the app is released.

## v0.1.0 (2026-10-27)
- Uploader API v1 for the Windows uploader app: pair the app through the browser, then it uploads your SavedVariables file by itself with its own upload-only token. Uploads from the app pass the same checks as the upload page and get the same session report; a block ends its tokens. (The uploader page follows.)
- The map on a phone fills the screen, with the roll call in a bottom sheet: the newest sightings peek above the tab bar, drag or tap the handle (or Filters) for the whole roll call with its filters. Tap a character on the map or in the roll call and the map zooms in while the sheet shows level, when seen, player, item level, role, spec, talents and professions, with "Open character" and the next character in that zone; Back or the arrow closes it. "4 in Kalimdor" at the edge flies to the other continent. Picking a continent on a phone no longer springs back to the whole world.
- The roll call next to the map: "9 of 10 located", who was seen where with the zone, the player (and "via" who relayed it) and how long ago, newest first under "New" and "Earlier". Filter by friend, New, faction and level; the map shows the same characters, and Back undoes a filter. Pick a character to fly the map there; the ones nobody has located yet and friends without the addon are listed at the bottom.
- Admin page (account menu › Admin, admin only): every member with the upload that added them and when they were last seen, block and unblock; the guest list, to add someone by BattleTag (refusing what isn't a BattleTag, a member or the admin), tick "May upload" or remove them; and who is blocked, by whom and when.
- Before the first upload the map says "The map is still empty", with "Upload the first file" for the admin and, for everyone else, who to wait for ("Vestras hasn't uploaded any data yet. Check back after the next session."); the roll call lists who is on the guest list. The roster and professions say the same. A page that takes a moment shows a thin gold line under the top bar, and the characters on the map fade in.
- Map zoom: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use + and − (and the arrow keys, + , − and 0 on the focused map). At 2× towns appear and small clusters fan out; at 4× dungeons and every character's name show, the zone in the centre is named in the top bar and the others dim. The map opens with the land centred, Azeroth / Kalimdor / Eastern Kingdoms fly there, and the switch follows when you pan to the other continent. Dragged past the edge it gives way only partly and springs back. A link to a character flies to it; "See them drop in on the map" in a session report drops that upload's new sightings in with a ripple.
- Sign-in gets its look: the community's name and "Where is everyone in Azeroth tonight?" over the drifting map, the Battle.net button and "We only read your BattleTag, nothing else". After Battle.net a short "Checking the guest list… Back from Battle.net as …" and then the map (or the page you opened). Someone not on the list sees "The gate stays closed" with their BattleTag, who to ask, and buttons to use another Battle.net account or sign out; when Battle.net doesn't answer, "Couldn't reach Battle.net" with "Try again".
- The footer credit is gone from every page; the credits come back in another place later.
- The character page: everything about one character. Name, level, race, class, faction and ruleset, who plays it, when it was last played and how fresh the data is; levels to 60 and the dungeon that fits the group; item level, role and main tree; talent bars; professions with a link to compare them with the group; the quest log; a small map of where it was last seen, with who else is there and a link to the map; and the player's other characters. Opens from the roster and the professions page.
- The world map on the home page: every friend's character at the zone where the addon last saw them, styled by how fresh the sighting is (glowing and pulsing when new, a ring after 3 days, dashed after a week), zones tinted gold by their latest sighting. Several characters in a zone form a cluster that fans out (or lists them, from six), with a badge when someone is in a dungeon. Point at or focus a character for a card with level, race, class, spec, zone, when seen, player and item level; click to select it (the link keeps it). Switch between Azeroth, Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms in the top bar.
- Professions: who can craft what across the group. A summary of the professions someone has at 225 or higher (covered), at 175–224 (thin) and below (gaps); a card per profession with everyone's skill bar, the room left until the current cap and the best starred; and a table of every character's professions. A card's title opens the roster filtered on that profession, a name opens the character. Recipe search follows once the addon shares known recipes.
- The roster as a table: every character on one line (player, level, race and class, role, talents, item level, professions, last played, last seen), sorted by clicking a column header (again to reverse), optionally grouped by friend, with the same search and filters as the cards; switching between cards and table keeps them. Your own characters are highlighted. On a phone the table shows as the card list, with filters and sort in a sheet.
- Session report: after an upload, and from every line of the upload history, a report of what changed: levels gained, skill gains (with "CAP REACHED"), new characters, new sightings, item level gains, and who sent data and how fresh it was. It rises in with the bars growing and the level ups shining (still without motion; "Replay" plays it again). Everyone signed in can open a report; a rejected upload, or one that changed nothing, says why.
- The roster: every friend with their characters as cards (class, level, race, talents, role, faction, professions, when last played, where last seen, item level), how fresh each friend's data is, who is around which level with the typical dungeon ranges, and quick answers (healers, tanks, level 30–40, level 60). Search, filter by class, role, level and profession, and sort; the choice is in the address, so a link shares it and Back undoes it.
- Upload page: drop `CommunitiesForever.lua` anywhere on the page or choose it (phones too). While it is read the page counts the characters and friends in it, then opens what the upload changed. A wrong file (another addon's), a damaged file, a file from a newer addon, the same data as before, someone else's file, a file of another community and a file over 25 MB each get a plain explanation and nothing changes. The history lists everyone's uploads with who sent them, what changed ("5 levels gained · 4 skill gains · 1 new character") or why it was rejected; times are shown in your own time zone.
- Atmosphere: drifting fog and light shafts on the map page, the drifting map with fog and embers behind sign-in, a quiet glow on the other pages. A "Motion" switch in the account menu turns all motion off (or on); it follows your system setting until you flip it and is remembered on this device.
- The account menu and the centre card are blurred over what's behind them again (the build had dropped the blur for Chrome), and screen readers hear the account button as "Account" plus the BattleTag shown.
- The map page sits on the darker map background.
- The site runs as a Docker image on TrueNAS behind nginx at https://wow.vestras.nl; `/api/health` tells whether it is up and which version runs.
- Sign in with Battle.net. Only the admin, members of the community (everyone named in an accepted upload) and guests on the admin's guest list get in; everyone else sees "You're not on the list". A blocked BattleTag is out at once.
- Uploads are accepted only for your own file of the same community: someone else's file is rejected as "not your file", one of another community as "other community".
- The site gets its look: the "Night over Azeroth" colours and type, a top bar with the community's name, Map / Roster / Professions, how old the data is, Upload and the account menu with sign out, a tab bar on phones, the footer credit, and a banner when the last upload is a week old or more.
- Map data: Thousand Needles is dry canyon again instead of the Cataclysm lake; Mount Hyjal, The Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Zephras Isle are zones on the map, so characters there (and in Krol'dok Stronghold, The Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit) are placed on it instead of "elsewhere".
