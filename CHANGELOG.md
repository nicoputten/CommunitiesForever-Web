## Unreleased

## v0.15.0 (2026-11-10)
- Mission pool v2 (owner only): a page you can read at a glance. A recipe bar says what a day and a week are made of, each slot with its pool and how often a mission comes back (orange when that's within about six weeks). Today's quarry gets its own card: the race and class to hunt, the other faction's target, how many Everloot's census saw this week and the next 7 days. Each day shows 6 lines: 3 PvE, the Hunt and the PvP slots. The pool is grouped in nine theme cards (Questing, Loot & gold, Social, Professions, Exploration, Combat & character, Skirmish, Weekly, Bounty) with their history; one opens below with its missions, How often and the switch. Search, Never came up, Hard and Off filter the whole pool. New page Hunt targets: every race and class of each faction weighted by Everloot's census, with the share of the draw; a click switches a target off or back on.
- Doomsday: pool version 6, with the addon of the same release. The site now decides every day's missions and sends them to the game 16 days ahead: 3 PvE dailies from 3 different themes (Questing, Loot & gold, Social, Professions, Exploration, Combat & character), the Hunt, a Skirmish and a Duel. 50 new PvP dailies join the Skirmish and the Duel slot, which now rotates through 8 duel missions; the 9 that still need a test on Forever start switched off. The Hunt adds the Skyborne and is weighted by the Everloot census (characters seen active in the last 7 days): a target seen fewer than 30 times sits out, and no target comes back within 7 days. The census is fetched once a day; if Everloot can't be reached, the last one is used. Six Hunt feats (First quarry, Quarry ×10, Every class, Big game, Rare quarry, Hunter’s week) come with the addon's Hunt kills. Set `EVERLOOT_API_KEY` on the server before the release.
- Doomsday: the Hunt gets its own look. Today shows the quarry as a red wanted card with the race and class, how many Everloot saw this week (with a link to its census), and a gold "Bagged" stamp with the time and zone once you got one. A new box, The Hunt today, lists who bagged one and shows tomorrow's quarry. The board has a Hunt column, the PvP tab a trophy collection of every race and class of the other faction plus this week's best hunters, and the Feats tab six Hunt feats with their progress.

## v0.5.0 (2026-10-29)

### Doomsday
Daily and weekly goals with points, a streak, feats and a ladder, in the game, on the site and in Discord. Goals are suggestions with points: skip any of them, nothing is locked. PvE and PvP are always side by side and never added together.
- **A new page, Doomsday** (`/doomsday`, or `/doom`), after Runs:
  - **Today:** your three PvE dailies and the Skirmish, each with a check and its time or the progress, the Duel slot, the time to the reset (19:00 realm time by default), "Today so far" (who wore the crown, who finished, who hasn't started) and the board of every friend against today's goals.
  - **Week:** the three weeklies and the Weekly bounty. **Board:** a 12-week heatmap of goals per friend.
  - **Ladder:** this week or all time, PvE and PvP each with its own ranking, movement since last week, and each friend opening to their characters. A friend who never killed anyone is a PvP Virgin: shown, never ranked.
  - **PvP:** per friend the kills this week, each character's PvP rank with our title, kills by zone, a duel grid with a duel rating, and battlegrounds apart.
  - **Feats:** the group's newest first with "First in the group", each friend's own with the next steps, and the PvP rank titles.
  - **On a phone:** your points and the reset under the tabs, goals as compact rows, the board as one card per friend and the ladder one track at a time. "Day one of Doomsday" before anyone has points.
- **Roster:** each friend's PvE and PvP points this week and their streak, or "No Doomsday data yet".
- **Feats:** every milestone is now a feat, and new ones join them: honorable kills, a new highest PvP rank with our title, duel wins, a first battleground win, 100 deaths, the first epic, mounts, the first catch, Jack of all trades and the profession watch list (Arcanite Reaper, Mooncloth, the Fishing Extravaganza and more). Special ones get a card image.
- **Discord:** the bot gets the daily board at each reset (kept up to date after uploads), the weekly ladder on Sunday, a `/doomsday` look-up and a Doomsday section in the weekly recap. The board and ladder posts start switched off; the admin turns them on at launch.
- **From the game:** the site keeps the new addon's (v0.8.0-beta1) PvP rank and kills, riding, deaths, duels, loot, play sessions, groups and each friend's goals. The reset time reaches the game through the uploader. There is no "Share my activity" any more: activity an older addon stopped sharing shows again.

### Weekly recap
- 21 new cards from the PvP, loot and play data (Warmonger, Battlemaster, Loot goblin, Duelist, Social butterfly, Bloodbath zone, Battleground of the week, Deadliest zone, Biggest group night, Rank race, Revenge, Duel rivals, Most deaths, Fell to their death, Died by fire, Untouchable, Big spender, Purple rain, Marathon, Night owl, Early bird), PvP and danger headlines, and five new challenges. Gold is never shown as an amount.

### Admin and Discord
- **Co-admins:** the owner can make friends admins, so they help run the group: guests, blocks, the Discord post settings, test posts and deleting any run. Starting fresh from launch, the bot token, appointing admins and revoking other friends' uploaders stay with the owner. "Admin changes" shows who changed what and when; the admin page shows who is online, and the owner sees every friend's uploader devices.
- **Start fresh from launch** (Danger zone, owner only): after you type `delete all`, the site forgets the beta (characters, sightings, activity, runs and asks from before it, recaps, feats and Doomsday). Nothing is deleted: who may sign in, Discord links, settings and uploaders stay, and Undo brings everything back.
- **The Discord page is reorganised:** "For you" (your Discord and time zone) and, for admins, one box per kind of post (Feats, Weekly recap, Runs, Doomsday), each with its own switch, channel and test post. Milestones are now called Feats and can have their own channel. The Doomsday box sets the daily board, the weekly ladder, their channel, the reset time (a change counts from the next reset, with Undo) and the realm's time zone.

- The roll call next to the map: "9 of 10 located", who was seen where with the zone, the player (and "via" who relayed it) and how long ago, newest first under "New" and "Earlier". Filter by friend, New, faction and level; the map shows the same characters, and Back undoes a filter. Pick a character to fly the map there; the ones nobody has located yet and friends without the addon are listed at the bottom.
- Admin page (account menu › Admin, admin only): every member with the upload that added them and when they were last seen, block and unblock; the guest list, to add someone by BattleTag (refusing what isn't a BattleTag, a member or the admin), tick "May upload" or remove them; and who is blocked, by whom and when.
- Before the first upload the map says "The map is still empty", with "Upload the first file" for the admin and, for everyone else, who to wait for ("Vestras hasn't uploaded any data yet. Check back after the next session."); the roll call lists who is on the guest list. The roster and professions say the same. A page that takes a moment shows a thin gold line under the top bar, and the characters on the map fade in.
- Map zoom: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use + and − (and the arrow keys, + , − and 0 on the focused map). At 2× towns appear and small clusters fan out; at 4× dungeons and every character's name show, the zone in the centre is named in the top bar and the others dim. The map opens with the land centred, Azeroth / Kalimdor / Eastern Kingdoms fly there, and the switch follows when you pan to the other continent. Dragged past the edge it gives way only partly and springs back. A link to a character flies to it; "See them drop in on the map" in a session report drops that upload's new sightings in with a ripple.
- Sign-in gets its look: the community's name and "Where is everyone in Azeroth tonight?" over the drifting map, the Battle.net button and "We only read your BattleTag, nothing else". After Battle.net a short "Checking the guest list… Back from Battle.net as …" and then the map (or the page you opened). Someone not on the list sees "The gate stays closed" with their BattleTag, who to ask, and buttons to use another Battle.net account or sign out; when Battle.net doesn't answer, "Couldn't reach Battle.net" with "Try again".

- The character page: everything about one character. Name, level, race, class, faction and ruleset, who plays it, when it was last played and how fresh the data is; levels to 60 and the dungeon that fits the group; item level, role and main tree; talent bars; professions with a link to compare them with the group; the quest log; a small map of where it was last seen, with who else is there and a link to the map; and the player's other characters. Opens from the roster and the professions page.
- The world map on the home page: every friend's character at the zone where the addon last saw them, styled by how fresh the sighting is (glowing and pulsing when new, a ring after 3 days, dashed after a week), zones tinted gold by their latest sighting. Several characters in a zone form a cluster that fans out (or lists them, from six), with a badge when someone is in a dungeon. Point at or focus a character for a card with level, race, class, spec, zone, when seen, player and item level; click to select it (the link keeps it). Switch between Azeroth, Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms in the top bar.
- Professions: who can craft what across the group. A summary of the professions someone has at 225 or higher (covered), at 175–224 (thin) and below (gaps); a card per profession with everyone's skill bar, the room left until the current cap and the best starred; and a table of every character's professions. A card's title opens the roster filtered on that profession, a name opens the character. Recipe search follows once the addon shares known recipes.
- The roster as a table: every character on one line (player, level, race and class, role, talents, item level, professions, last played, last seen), sorted by clicking a column header (again to reverse), optionally grouped by friend, with the same search and filters as the cards; switching between cards and table keeps them. Your own characters are highlighted. On a phone the table shows as the card list, with filters and sort in a sheet.
- Session report: after an upload, and from every line of the upload history, a report of what changed: levels gained, skill gains (with "CAP REACHED"), new characters, new sightings, item level gains, and who sent data and how fresh it was. It rises in with the bars growing and the level ups shining (still without motion; "Replay" plays it again). Everyone signed in can open a report; a rejected upload, or one that changed nothing, says why.
- The roster: every friend with their characters as cards (class, level, race, talents, role, faction, professions, when last played, where last seen, item level), how fresh each friend's data is, who is around which level with the typical dungeon ranges, and quick answers (healers, tanks, level 30–40, level 60). Search, filter by class, role, level and profession, and sort; the choice is in the address, so a link shares it and Back undoes it.
- Upload page: drop `CommunitiesForever.lua` anywhere on the page or choose it (phones too). While it is read the page counts the characters and friends in it, then opens what the upload changed. A wrong file (another addon's), a damaged file, a file from a newer addon, the same data as before, someone else's file, a file of another community and a file over 25 MB each get a plain explanation and nothing changes. The history lists everyone's uploads with who sent them, what changed ("5 levels gained · 4 skill gains · 1 new character") or why it was rejected; times are shown in your own time zone.
- Atmosphere: drifting fog and light shafts on the map page, the drifting map with fog and embers behind sign-in, a quiet glow on the other pages. A "Motion" switch in the account menu turns all motion off (or on); it follows your system setting until you flip it and is remembered on this device.
