Tooltips & menus
  1. ⓘ next to “No known source”: Not in our data yet.
  2. Hover on a crafted link: opens that recipe in the drawer (Back returns here)
  3. Hover on “limited stock”: The vendor sells a few, then restocks.
Window chrome (title, tabs) not listed.

Rules

Where: under each reagent in the recipe drawer (RC04), indented to the name. Same on phone inside the recipe sheet.
At most 3 sources, best first: 1. vendor without limit · 2. crafted, when someone in the group knows that recipe · 3. gathered · 4. vendor with limited stock · 5. crafted, nobody knows it · 6. drop, by chance. Ties: nearest level first.
Shown here: Heavy Leather with 3 mixed sources · Cured Heavy Hide crafted (the link swaps the drawer to that recipe, with a Back arrow) · Silken Thread with 1 vendor · Guardian Stone with no data (“No known source” + ⓘ).
Lines are 13 px, one line each, wrapping only when a creature or NPC name is long. Gold is never shown, so no vendor prices.
Vendor · NPC · zone · “limited stock” tag when it applies
Drop · up to 2 creatures with their chance, highest first
Skinning · creature type and level range
Herbalism · the herb node
Mining · the ore node
Crafted · link to the recipe that makes it + its profession and skill
