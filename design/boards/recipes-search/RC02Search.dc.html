Reused & rules

The existing Professions page stays as designed: summary (covered · thin · gaps), By profession cards with the crafter bars and badges, By character table. Only two changes:
1. The search goes live. The dashed “future” box and its line (“Recipe search arrives when…”) are replaced by the real search field in the same place. Results from 2 letters, typo-tolerant (MiniSearch over item and recipe names), max 6 under Can craft and 3 under Nobody yet, best quality first. A result = item icon, name in quality colour, then the crafters (class colour + difficulty dot) or, when nobody knows it, where it’s learned. Click or Enter opens the profession page with that recipe’s drawer open.
2. Each crafting card (Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Cooking, First Aid) gets one line at the bottom, “112 of 185 recipes ›”, linking to /professions/tailoring. Gathering cards and Fishing stay as they are.
