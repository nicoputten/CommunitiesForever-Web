Step 1 · five names for the feature

One name for goals, feats and the PvP ladder together. Points stay simply “points” (PvE 85 · PvP 240). The favourite is used on every screen that follows; swapping it later is a find-and-replace.

Doomsday

Every day is Doomsday: fresh goals at 09:00, glory by bedtime, a ladder on Sunday.

Favourite
The Grindstone

Proud of the grind. Sharpen your days on it; the ladder shows who never logs off.

Deeds of Doom

Every quest, kill and ding written into the Doombringers’ book of deeds.

The Doom Tally

Little lines on a shield, one per goal. Count them, then brag about them.

Bragging Rites

The sacred rites: goals in the morning, feats at night, gloating on Sunday.

Why Doomsday

It is about the day (the 09:00 reset is the heartbeat), it has our name in it, and it is funny without trying: “Doomsday is done for Mira at 19:30”.

How it reads

Addon tab “Doomsday” · site page /doomsday · Discord /doomsday (short alias /doom) · “Doomsday · Tue 28 Oct” · “Doomsday ladder · week 44”.

Avoided

“Achievements” (Blizzard's own system), “Honor” (the game's PvP system) and anything that sounds like a chore list.
