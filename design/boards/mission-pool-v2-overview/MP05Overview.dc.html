Tooltips & menus
  1. ⓘ next to “seen this week”: Everloot census: characters seen in the last 7 days, from players who share uploads. A sample, not the whole realm.
  2. Hunt targets ›: opens the target grid (board MP06)
  3. Theme card click: opens that theme’s missions below the cards; one theme open at a time
  4. Rare / Normal / Often and the switch work as today
Window chrome (title, tabs) not listed.

What changes, what stays

Stays as built (PR #226): the header, Owner only, Next 14 days with lock icons, “changed” badge and the day editor, Next 2 weeks, Rare/Normal/Often and the on/off switch per mission, “since launch”.
1 · Recipe bar replaces the count chips: what one day and one week are made of, each slot with its pool size and how often a mission comes back. A slot that repeats within ~6 weeks (Bounty) turns orange.
2 · Today’s quarry: the Hunt gets its own card above the calendar: race + class portrait, +5, done today, how many of that combination Everloot saw this week, the other faction’s target, the next 7 targets, and a link to the target grid.
3 · Day tiles grow from 4 to 6 lines: 3 PvE (green), the Hunt (crosshair, race and class), 2 PvP (red). Same tile, same locks.
4 · The pool as themes: nine cards (Questing, Loot & gold, Social, Professions, Exploration, Combat & character, Skirmish, Weekly, Bounty) with count and history; one opens below into compact rows. The four stat columns merge into one “History” line (“came up 2× · 75% · Tue”). Filters on top: Never came up, Hard (<30% done), Off.
Assumption: nothing is removed from the 108 PvE missions; the themes are new tags on each mission, and the draw takes the 3 PvE from 3 different themes so a day never has two quest missions.
