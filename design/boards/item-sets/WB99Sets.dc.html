Rules

Order inside the tooltip: the item’s own lines → a 10 px gap → set name (0/N) in gold → the set’s items, one per line, indented 10 px → 10 px gap → bonuses “(2) Set: …” in set-piece order → our own footer (source, “Nobody has it”) under a hairline.
Nothing is worn: the site doesn’t know who wears what, so the count is always 0/N and every piece and bonus is grey (#808080), exactly the game’s “not worn” look. No item is highlighted, not even the one you’re hovering.
Width stays 300–320 px on desktop (the 8-piece set makes it taller, not wider). On phone it fills the sheet; long sets scroll inside the sheet.
Texts (set name, items, bonuses) come from the client files; the examples here are illustrative.
