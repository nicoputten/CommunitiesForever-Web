Rules

✓One image per day, re-rendered and swapped in place when an upload changes something (edit the message, replace the attachment). At most once every 5 minutes.
✓Text line above the image carries the news, so notifications and screen readers get it without the image.
✓Our own check boxes in the image (green PvE, red PvP) instead of ✅ and ▢ emoji; done names struck through, crown for first.
✓Readable at 300 px on a phone: 30 px names, 44 px boxes, one-word column labels (Level, 3 quests, Skills, Hunt, Lone wolf); full goal titles are in the embed text and on the site.
✓No legend line under the post; the headers are in the image.
✓Friends without the addon or without Doomsday data are left out of the image; the text says “2 friends without addon data”.
✓Fallback without image: the old text table, minus the leaked legend.
