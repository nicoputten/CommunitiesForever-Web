Doomsday · rules & catalogue

The one page every screen follows. Goals recommend, never restrict. Two tracks that are never added together.

Two tracks

PvE
quests, levels, dungeons, professions, playing together
PvP
kills, duels, the hunt
Always side by side: PvE 85 · PvP 240. Each has its own ranking; a friend who never does PvP is never ranked against gankers. Any of your characters counts and progress adds up across them.

Schedule

Day resets 09:00 · week resets Sunday 09:00
Our own clock, not the game's weekly reset or honor week.
Shown as “resets in 3 h 12 m” on Today, Week and the Discord board.
Each day 3 PvE dailies · Hunt · Skirmish · open Duel slot
Each week 3 PvE weeklies · 1 PvP bounty

Streak & swaps

6 days · best 11 = days in a row with at least one goal done, either track.
A goal you can't do (all characters 60, all secondary professions at 300) is swapped for you only. Small blue mark; the original sits in the tooltip: “Instead of: Raise Cooking, First Aid or Fishing”.
