What makes it look like the game

Tree panel: the Forever Destruction tree (3 × 7, 16 talents, from the client via Everloot) on a dark fire-and-shadow background, square icons with a 2 px border: gold glow = full rank, green = partly ranked, grey = open, desaturated = not reachable yet (row gate or missing prerequisite). Each icon has the game’s rank box bottom-right (“5/5” gold, “2/5” green, “0/3” grey).
Prerequisite arrows: Intensity → Pyroclasm, Conflagrate → Fire and Brimstone, Bane of Havoc → Incinerate (from the client’s prerequisites). Gold when the first talent has points, grey otherwise.
Talent tooltip in the game’s format: name · “Rank 2/5” · current text in gold · “Next rank:” + next text · red “Requires 20 points in Destruction Talents” / “Requires Intensity” when locked.
Tree tooltip (hover a tree row): the small grid on the left, the ranked talents on the right with icon, name, gold text and a rank pill.
Icons are stand-ins drawn here; the site uses the real spell icons from the icon cache (the same cache as items). Texts are illustrative; the site shows the client’s spell descriptions for the rank.
