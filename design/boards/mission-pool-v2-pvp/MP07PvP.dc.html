easy = the addon already sees this (honorable kills, the killed player when targeted or moused over like the Hunt, duel results from the system message, flagged state, zone, group, level, time). check = needs a test on Forever first (health or target of target during combat, guards and NPC kills, assists), because combat details may be hidden from addons.

Names are short like the existing ones so they fit the day tiles (max ~12 characters).
