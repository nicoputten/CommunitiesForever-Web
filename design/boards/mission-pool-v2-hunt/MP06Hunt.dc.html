How the Hunt uses Everloot

Valid targets come from the game files via Everloot (which races can be which class in WoW: Forever). That adds High Order Skyborne (Alliance: Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Mage, Druid seen) and keeps Forever’s own combos like Dwarf Shaman. Today’s list (23 Vanilla combos) misses them; with Everloot it’s 28 valid combos, of which 25 are common enough to draw.
Weight = how many of that race and class Everloot’s census saw active in the last 7 days. Human Paladin (435) comes up about 11× as often as Night Elf Warrior (65), so a Hunt is usually doable. Combos under 30 seen (Dwarf Rogue 20, Skyborne Rogue 15, Skyborne Warrior 10) are skipped until they grow.
Refresh once a day before the draw for days that aren’t locked yet (today and tomorrow keep their target). The census is a sample (players who share uploads, here 13 installs), so it’s a weight, not a promise; the grid says so under it.
Owner override: click a cell to switch a target off or back on; the day editor (MP02) can still pin a target on a day.
Numbers on this board are Everloot’s real census for 4–10 Nov 2026 (rounded to 5). More: everloot.app/census
