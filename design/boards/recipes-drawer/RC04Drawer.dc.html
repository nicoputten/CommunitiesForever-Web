Tooltip rules

Built from our own item data (wago.tools Classic tables, by item id): name in quality colour, bind, slot and type on one line, armor, stats, durability, required level, green Equip/Use lines. No sell price (gold is never shown).
Looks like the game: dark blue-black, 1 px grey-blue border, 13 px text, 300 px wide (reagents 240). It’s the one place the site copies the game look on purpose.
Icons come from the icon cache on the server (fetched once per icon, served by us). Border colour = quality.
Phone: no hover; tap the icon to open the tooltip in a sheet (board RC05).
