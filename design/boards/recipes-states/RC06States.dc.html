Rules

Nobody has = no character in the group knows it (removed or unobtainable recipes are left out by the import). Filtered to a character shows “as of …” only when that scan is over 7 days old; it opens on the Known tab, which then means “Ves knows it” (86). No results keeps the filters visible. A profession whose crafters never opened it in game says “No recipes scanned yet”; “See all recipes” still lists every recipe as Nobody has.
