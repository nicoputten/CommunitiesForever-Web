Reused & rules

Reused: the dungeon page as it is (header, breadcrumb, hero, Who fits, Planned here, Ran here) and its .box; the character rows use the round class portrait plus the full name in class colour, like Who fits and the Roster.
Placement: desktop: left column, directly under Who fits (same width, 838 px). Phone: one column, after Who fits and before Planned here.
Rows: one per quest, sorted by how many characters have it. The zone is the quest log header the quest sits under, in grey; left out when unknown.
Overflow: desktop shows 4 characters, phone 3; the rest go into a “+N” chip. Hover, focus or tap shows the names; nothing moves.
Empty: the box stays, with one line, so players know the feature exists.
