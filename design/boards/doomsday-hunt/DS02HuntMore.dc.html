Rules

Board: the Hunt column gets a faint red background and the quarry portrait in its header. A kill shows a crosshair and the time; “PvP Virgin” stays for friends who never fought.
PvP tab: next to Kills / Duels / Battlegrounds, a Hunt trophies collection per friend (the same friend chips choose whose): every valid Alliance race × class from the game files (via Everloot), gold when bagged with the count. Targets under 60 seen this week get a blue “rare” ring. Plus Hunt · this week: days with the quarry bagged, per friend.
Feats: First quarry, Quarry ×10, Every class, Big game (all 28), Rare quarry (a target under 1% of Everloot’s census), Hunter’s week (7 days in a row). They post to the group feed like the other feats.
Data: the addon already records Hunt kills (target or mouse over); the zone comes from where the kill happened. Census numbers are Everloot’s sample (13 installs), shown as “seen this week”.
