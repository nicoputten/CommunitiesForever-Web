What changes on /doomsday (Today)

Stays: the header stats, the six tabs, Your goals with 3 PvE + Hunt + Skirmish + Duel slot, Today so far, the reset line.
The Hunt card stands out in its slot: a red wanted-style card with a faint crosshair, the race + class portrait and the target in Marcellus (“Night Elf Hunter” instead of “Hunt: Night Elf Hunters”), “+5 PvP” and how many Everloot saw this week. Done: gold border, “Bagged” stamp, time and zone of your kill.
The Hunt today (new, right column above Today so far): who bagged one and where, “1 of 12”, and tomorrow’s quarry (tomorrow is already locked, so showing it is safe and lets people plan).
Today so far gets the kill as a line (“Grom bagged a Night Elf Hunter”).
Names on these boards are made up.
