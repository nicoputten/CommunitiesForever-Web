Tested on wow.vestras.nl on 27 Oct 2026 as Vane: Runs list, run detail (organiser), Edit run, Plan a run and the roster bands. No answers or runs were submitted, because those post to Discord. Not tested: answering as someone else, phone widths, the Discord buttons.

The three open damage slots each say Khrono Wolfhowl could: the sentence is cut off and the same name repeats in every slot.

→ Slots say only Open. One line under the group lists who could fill them: Could fill: Khrono Wolfhowl, Ginji Shadowstep, Basil Stonegaze.

Plan a run disables Twinkle Stardust 10 (too low). On the run page the same character is selectable as 16 levels short.

→ One rule for both pages (see the level fit rules on the picker board).

3

Vesgar Bloodhoof is always tank. Nobody can join as damage, and the organiser can't move a friend into the open healer slot.

→ Character and role are picked separately. The organiser can move people between slots.
