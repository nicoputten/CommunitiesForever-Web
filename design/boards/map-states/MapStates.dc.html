Zoom levels

World · 1×
Both continents. One marker per character, a cluster per zone when there are 2 or more. Capitals only. Zone labels at full size.
Continent · 2×
Town dots and names appear (Crossroads, Booty Bay…). Clusters of 2–5 open as a fan by default. Labels hold their screen size.
Zone · 4×
Dungeon icons with names, every marker gets a name tag. The zone name moves to the top bar; other zones dim slightly.
