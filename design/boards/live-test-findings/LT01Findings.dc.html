Bugs · 7

bugPvP Virgin next to PvP points · Tok shows “PvP 3” and “PvP Virgin” on Roster, board and ladder.
→ Decided: any PvP point ends PvP Virgin (duels included).
bugWrong rank title · PvP page: “Conscientious Objector” with 1 kill.
→ Only with 0 PvP points ever; otherwise “No rank yet · 1 kill”.
bugCancelled run under “Past” · Runs: tomorrow’s cancelled run is listed in Past.
→ Past = start time before now. Cancelled future runs stay in This week, struck through, for 24 h.
bugGoal count · Today: “2 of 4 done” with 5 goals on screen.
→ Count every goal shown (3 PvE + skirmish + duel slot = 5) or label “2 of 3 dailies”.
bugDay label vs 19:00 reset · “Wed 28 Oct” with checks at 00:04 and 03:27.
→ Decided: “Wed evening · 19:00 → Thu 19:00”; times after midnight say “Thu 00:04”.
bugMap age vs Roster · Map: “6 d” on every marker. Roster: “seen 42 min ago”.
→ Decided: no age badge under 1 day; older locations fade and say “here 6 d ago” on hover.
bugDuel grid · “0–1” wraps, scrollbar in the card, you vs yourself.
→ Names as row and column, diagonal “—”, nowrap scores, fits without scrolling up to 8 friends.
