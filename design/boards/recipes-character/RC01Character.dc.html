Reused & rules

Same box as today (bars, numbers, “Compare with the group →”). New: a line under each crafting profession, “86 recipes ›”, linking to /professions/tailoring?char=ves-doombringer (the profession page filtered to this character).
Gathering and Fishing keep the bar only (none on Ves). Cooking and First Aid get the recipes line like crafting.
“as of 21 Oct” appears only when that profession’s last scan is over 7 days old. Never scanned: “Recipes not scanned” + ⓘ, no link.
Specialisation (Mooncloth, Dragonscale…) shows as a small chip on that line once the scan reports one.
