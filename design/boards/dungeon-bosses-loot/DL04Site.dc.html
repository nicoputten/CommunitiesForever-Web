Reused & rules

The dungeon page stays (hero, Who fits, Quests, Planned here, Ran here). New: Bosses & loot between Who fits and Quests, and a Bosses jump list at the top of the right column (sticky on scroll; the boss in view is bold).
Boss sections all open, in DungeonEncounter order (optional bosses say “optional”): number + name, the boss’s own drops at 1 % or more (plus its quest and recipe drops at any chance), max 6, then Show 2 more ▾; loot in two columns: icon (quality border), name in quality colour, type, drop % (highest first). Tags: Quest (quest-only drop) and Nobody knows it (recipe nobody in the group has). A recipe drop links to its recipe drawer.
Usable by [character ▾]: defaults to your most recently played character; dims what it can’t use, the switch hides it. One Classic data ⓘ per section.
Ran here now says “Last cleared Tue” + the friends in that group: cleared = the final boss died (VanCleef); optional bosses don’t matter. Planned here shows boss progress once the run has started.
Wings: each wing is its own dungeon page with its own bosses (the wing chips on the hero stay). Raids aren’t in this version.
